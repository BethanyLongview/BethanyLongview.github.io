## A Little About Susan

Susan brings together a wide range of gifts and interests — art and Bible art-journaling, writing, public speaking, and a lifelong love of learning. Her spiritual life is shaped by practices like centering prayer, spiritual direction, and walking the labyrinth. She holds a deep love for the Pacific Northwest.

## Family

Susan and her husband met as students in Pullman through marching band. He is now Associate Professor of Music at the University of Portland, where he directs the bands, and is also Music Director of the Southwest Washington Wind Symphony. Together they have three children — two daughters and a son.
