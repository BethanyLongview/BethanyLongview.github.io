Bethany's building is a resource for the whole community. We host weddings, funerals, memorials, receptions, meetings, and study groups, with a church coordinator on hand to help plan your event.

## Spaces Available

### Sanctuary

Seats up to **150**. Available for weddings, funerals, and other ceremonies. Equipped with piano, organ, and microphones. A musician can be arranged, and a sound technician is available for a small fee.

### Fellowship Hall

Seats up to **120**. Well suited for receptions, parties, celebrations, and larger meetings. Full kitchen access including refrigerator, double-oven stove, and dishwasher. A portable coffee station is available.

### Faith Room

Seats up to **15**. A smaller classroom-style space for study groups, classes, and craft activities.

## Weddings & Funerals

Pastor Susan is available to officiate both on-site and off-site weddings and funeral services. Our space, music resources, and Fellowship Hall make Bethany a meaningful setting for life's most important gatherings.

## Pricing & Policies

- Members receive discounted rates.
- Non-member rentals start at **$150**.
- Catering is available through the **Women of Bethany** at additional cost.
- Alcohol is permitted at some events with prior authorization.
