We worship together for **Word and Sacrament every Sunday morning at 9:30 a.m.**, followed by fellowship time. Our music blends hymns new and old, contemporary praise songs, and modern Christian music.

## Holy Communion

We celebrate the sacrament of Holy Communion as part of regular Sunday worship. *[Cadence and practice — needs confirmation from Pastor Susan.]*

## Special Sundays

- **Small Seeds and Sprouts Sunday** — On the third Sunday of the month, our children and youth help lead worship, reading lessons, lighting candles, and assisting with communion.
- **Fifth Sundays** — When a month has five Sundays, our Worship & Music team leads a lay-led service exploring varied themes, giving congregation members space to share their faith.

## Joining Online

In-person worship is at 9:30 a.m. at 2900 Parkview Dr. Services are also available online via Zoom and Facebook. *[Zoom meeting ID and Facebook page link — needs current details.]*

## What to Expect

If it's your first Sunday with us:

- Come as you are — dress is casual to business casual.
- Parking is in the main lot off Parkview Drive; the sanctuary entrance is straight ahead.
- An usher will hand you a bulletin and welcome you. Sit anywhere that looks comfortable.
- Children are welcome in worship. *[Nursery availability — needs confirmation.]*
