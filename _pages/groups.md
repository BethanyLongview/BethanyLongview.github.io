Life at Bethany happens all week long. Beyond Sunday worship, we gather for study, fellowship, prayer, and service — across seasons, ages, and stages of faith. Whether you love digging into scripture, lending a hand, making music, or simply sharing a meal, there's a place for you here. Visitors are welcome at any of our ongoing groups.

## Moments at Bethany

A weekly time for scripture and spiritual conversation. **Wednesdays at 1:30 p.m.** — lakeside during warmer months, in the Faith Room when the weather turns colder.

## Sunday Bible Study

A welcoming, spirit-filled group that gathers with Pastor Susan to dig into God's Word together. Come with your questions, your insights, and your curiosity — it's a wonderful way to grow in faith and in friendship. All are welcome, wherever you are on your journey.

## Breakfast Group

An informal weekly breakfast gathering. **Tuesdays at 8:00 a.m.** at Corner Cafe on Commerce Street, Longview. Contact Bob or the church office for details.

## Women's Bible Study

We meet on **Tuesday mornings at 10:30 a.m.** to pray, read scripture, and share together, in the Faith Room and on Zoom. Participants bring their [*Gather* magazine](https://www.gathermagazine.org/). Our study is part of [Women of the ELCA (WELCA)](https://www.womenoftheelca.org/). Contact Pastor Susan or the church office.

## Praise Team

Come, musicians and singers, and join Dorothy McMillan each Sunday to lead our music and help worship feel alive. From hymns to contemporary praise, this gifted group brings energy and joy to our gatherings. If you sing or play an instrument and would like to share your gifts, we'd love to have you — talk with Dorothy or the church office.

## FISH Food Distributions

Bethany partners in the community effort to feed our neighbors through **FISH** food distributions, providing groceries to families and individuals facing hard times across Cowlitz County. It's a hands-on way to live out our calling to care for one another.

The next scheduled distribution dates are **September 14 and November 6**. To learn more about food assistance and the agencies we partner with, visit [Lower Columbia CAP](https://lowercolumbiacap.org/). Want to help? Contact the church office to volunteer.

## Duct Tape Team

Every church needs a crew that keeps things running — and ours is the Duct Tape Team, our building and grounds ministry. These dedicated volunteers handle landscaping, repairs, and the everyday upkeep that keeps Bethany beautiful, safe, and welcoming. No special skills required — just a willingness to lend a hand. We need volunteers! Whether you can swing a hammer, pull a few weeds, or help with seasonal projects, you'll be part of a friendly, get-it-done team. Contact the church office to join in.

## Hospitality

Behind every warm welcome and shared meal is our **Hospitality** team. They host our events and gatherings — providing food, beverages, decorations, and that all-important friendly face — so everyone feels at home at Bethany. From coffee hour after worship to receptions and seasonal celebrations, this team turns ordinary moments into times of genuine fellowship. New hands are always welcome; contact the church office to get involved.

## Seasonal & Special Events

Seasonal services, holiday events, and special programs are announced in the [Weekly Announcements](/announcements/) and the [Monthly Newsletter](/newsletter-archive/).
