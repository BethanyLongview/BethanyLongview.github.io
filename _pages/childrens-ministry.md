> *Small seeds can lead to great things.*

At Bethany we invite our youngest disciples onto their own journeys of faith — through worship, scripture, fellowship, and a lot of fun together.

## Small Seeds (Elementary)

Games, Bible study, crafts, and time together for our elementary-aged kids. We meet **Saturdays at 12:30 p.m.** for about an hour and a half.

## Sprouts (Middle & High School)

Our middle and high school youth dig into scripture, ask hard questions, and grow in faith alongside friends. Sprouts also meets **Saturdays at 12:30 p.m.** and forms the core of our **three-year confirmation program**.

## Small Seeds and Sprouts Sunday

On the **third Sunday of each month**, kids and youth help lead our 9:30 a.m. worship — reading lessons, lighting candles, and assisting with communion. It's a chance for the whole congregation to experience worship through the eyes of our youngest members.
