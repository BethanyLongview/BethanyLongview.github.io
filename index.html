Summer Surge Series

Wednesdays at 6:00 p.m. · Public Welcome

Join us all summer for stories, music, crafts, and conversation. Sign up by calling 360-577-8240.

🔥July 8

Campfire Stories with S'mores

Pastor Susan leads us in storytelling, and all are welcome to share their favorite camping stories. S'mores will be served around the campfire — bring your own lawn chair and join us!

🎥July 15

Film Festival Short Films

Carl Winston has produced several short films shown at U.S. film festivals — sci-fi, Sasquatch, drama, and a documentary. Come listen as Carl shares his experiences.

🥛July 22

Craft Night with Oyster Shells

Nancy Skora leads us in using oyster shells to make home décor. Come show your creative side and make new friends! Craft fee: $2.00.

🍦July 29

Old Fashioned Hymn Sing with Ice Cream Sundaes

Talented Dorothy McMillan will be “tickling the ivory keys” and leading us in favorite old-time hymns — bring your ideas! Ice cream sundaes served. Free will offering collected.

📱Aug 5

Security: Scams, Hacking & More

Barb Clausen guides a hands-on activity followed by discussion, and a local specialist shares current scams and trends to watch for in our area.

🚶Aug 12

Camino Portugal Pilgrimage

Dr. Phyllis Cavens returns from walking the Portugal portion of the Camino toward Santiago de Compostela, Spain. Come along as she shares what she learned as a Pilgrim on the Way.

Life at Bethany

Where Faith Meets Community

Worship

Word and Sacrament every Sunday at 9:30 a.m., with music that blends hymns, contemporary praise, and modern Christian songs.

Children & Youth

Small Seeds (elementary) and Sprouts (middle & high school) meet Saturdays. Confirmation runs as a three-year journey.

Sermons

Catch up on Sunday or revisit a favorite — full services and sermons are archived on YouTube.

Groups

Bible study, breakfast group, women's group, praise team, and service teams — there's a way in for every season of life.

Weddings & Facility Use

Sanctuary, Fellowship Hall, and Faith Room are available for weddings, funerals, receptions, and community meetings.

Prayer Requests

Share a prayer concern privately with Pastor Susan and the Bethany prayer team.
