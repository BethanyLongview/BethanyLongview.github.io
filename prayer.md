Use this form to share a prayer request with Pastor Susan and the Bethany prayer team.

**Privacy:** Your request is shared only with Pastor Susan and the prayer team — it is not published on this website or anywhere public. You may leave your name and email blank to submit anonymously. Use the checkboxes below to tell us whether your request is private for Pastor Susan only, whether you'd like to join the prayer team, and whether it may be included in Sunday's bulletin.

If your request is urgent, please call the church office directly.
